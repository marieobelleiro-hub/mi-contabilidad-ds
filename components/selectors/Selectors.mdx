# Selectors

Small controls that **show or change a state**:

- **Interactive:** checkbox and radio, the clickable status dot, the switch (toggle).
- **Static:** the status dot on its own, and the chips (labels you can't click).

## Choice card

A radio or checkbox option shown as a card, when each option needs a title and a short explanation. Same look, different syntax:

- **Radio: one choice.** The options are exclusive. Real case: **Guardar copia .html** (Local app), *Versión actual (con datos)* / *Versión vacía (sin datos)*.
- **Checkbox: several choices.** Any number of cards can be on. Not in the app yet.

The selected card gets the accent border and tint; hover shows the accent border; the focus ring goes around the whole card.

## States

Checkbox, radio and switch: **selected / deselected** (on / off), **focus** and **disabled**; no hover change, the pointer over them is enough. The choice card also has **hover** (accent border), and its focus ring goes around the whole card. The clickable status dot: **Cobrada / Pendiente**, **hover** (grey circle) and **focus**. ## Accessibility

- **Checkbox & radio** are native `<input>`s inside a `<label>`, so clicking the text works too. A hidden label stays as `aria-label`.
- **Status dot.** A dot that carries meaning needs 3:1 contrast with its background (WCAG 1.4.11). Orange passes (5.5:1) but green on white is only 1.3:1, so **every dot has a 1px `--text-muted` ring** (5.7:1): clickable, static and in legends: the dot is always visible, whatever its colour. Colour still tells paid from pending, so the words are always there too: the `aria-label` ("Cobrada. Pulsa para cambiar") and the legend above the list.
- **Switch / toggle** is a `<button role="switch" aria-checked>` (`switch__control`), read as "English, switch, off". The accessible name says what turning it on does.
- The two labels (Esp / Eng) sit **outside the button**, hidden from screen readers. If they were inside, the visible text ("Esp Eng") and the name ("English") wouldn't match, and voice-control users saying "click Eng" couldn't reach it (WCAG 2.5.3, *label in name*). Clicking a label still works: it sets that side.
- Hiding the visible labels keeps the accessible name.
- **Choice cards** are native `<input>`s inside a `<label>`: the whole card is clickable. Group them in a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>` (can be visually hidden) so screen readers announce what is being chosen. Radio cards share one `name`.
- **Chips** are plain text: not focusable, not clickable. If it needs to be clicked, use a Tab or a Button.
