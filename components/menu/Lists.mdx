# Lists & menus

Components that **put several inputs or options together**. Three components, the same as in Figma:

| Component | What it is | In the app |
|---|---|---|
| [Form](#form) | a list of fields, 20px apart | the mobile forms Nueva factura · Nuevo gasto (inside the full-screen <a href="./?path=/docs/app-components-dialog--dialog#mobile-forms" target="_top">Dialog</a>) |
| [Selector group](#selector-group) | checkboxes, radios or choice cards under one legend | Guardar copia .html (choice cards) |
| [Menu](#menu) | the open list of a dropdown: text, radio or checkbox options | **not yet**: see the note |

## Form

The fields of a form in their order, 20px apart (`form`), in the colour of the section (`form--primary` / `form--attention`). **Tinted** is for the mobile forms: they fill the screen with no card around them, so the tint shows where to type. In the app a form always lives inside the full-screen dialog, with its title, Cancelar and Guardar.

The mobile forms start with the **quarter dropdown** (the grey <a href="./?path=/docs/app-components-tab--docs" target="_top">Tab · selector</a>): the quarter the record is saved in, so it can be changed without leaving the form. They show a **live total** (`field__total`, 14px bold capitals, in the form's colour): **Total factura** = base + IVA − 15 % IRPF retention (exenta: the base) · **Total gasto** = base + IVA. If the date is not in the chosen quarter, the Atención! alert appears **before** saving and the form stays open.

## Selector group

Several selectors under one title (`<fieldset>` + `<legend>`): **checkboxes** when you can choose several, **radios** or **choice cards** when you choose one. The legend can be hidden; it stays for screen readers. The options are the real ones of the app (% de IVA, Categoría IRPF, the two versions of Guardar copia .html).

## Menu

> **Not in the app yet.** The app's dropdowns (% de IVA, Categoría IRPF) use the **browser's own menu**: the open list of a `<select>` is drawn by the browser, or by the phone's picker, and can't be styled. This menu is ready for when the app needs a custom dropdown. The only custom menu in the app today is the mobile <a href="./?path=/docs/app-components-tab--tab" target="_top">Tab · selector</a>, which uses the same panel (white, `--shadow-md`).

Three kinds of option, the same as a selector group: **text** (one choice, the selected one tinted), **radio** (one choice) and **checkbox** (several). Try it: click an option, or use ↑ ↓ and Enter.

## Spec

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Form | `form` · `--space-20` between fields · colour `form--primary` / `--attention` · `form--tinted` |
| Selector group | `check-group` (8px gap) · `choice-group` (8px gap) · legend Sans 14 |
| Menu · panel | `--surface` · `--shadow-md` · 4px top and bottom · min width `--menu-min-w` 240px · taller than `--menu-max-h` 320px: scrolls |
| Menu · option | `--menu-item-h` 40px (the height of a field) · 12px sides · Sans 14 (16 on mobile) · radio / checkbox 20px, 12px before the text |
| Hover | `--fill-hover` |
| Selected (text) | `--field-tint` + medium weight |
| Disabled | `--disabled` text, no hover |
| Focus | yellow `--focus` ring inside the option + black halo |

## Accessibility

- **Form**: every field has its `<label>`; the order on screen is the order of the keyboard.
- **Selector group**: a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>`, so screen readers say what is being chosen ("% de IVA, group"). Radios share one `name`.
- **Menu · text**: `role="listbox"` with `role="option"` and `aria-selected`; the trigger has `aria-haspopup="listbox"` and `aria-expanded`. Keyboard: ↑ ↓ move, Enter / Space choose, Esc closes and returns to the trigger.
- **Menu · radio / checkbox**: native inputs in a `<fieldset>` with a (hidden) `<legend>`.
- Before building a custom dropdown, remember what the browser's `<select>` gives for free: the phone's picker, the keyboard and screen readers.
